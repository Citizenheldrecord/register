R-2026-10-25-B. Cost grade follows the cost cell (hype, means and cost rule 7)

Entered 25 October 2026. When a resolved item's cost cell reads "FAILED: no public figure; held by [holder]", the COST grade is FAILED: the holder had not published the cost by the scoring date (failure protocol rule 5: unverifiable and still OPEN on the scoring date is scored FAILED, with the reason). COST stays OPEN only where the cost cell itself is OPEN or a sourced figure exists but the time or foregone lines are unsourced. Not retroactive to entries scored before 20 October 2026 (HR5334 stays OPEN). Grades are corrected by new dated grade lines, never by editing a row. First grade lines: CORRECTIONS/2026-10-25-grade-lines.md.

R-2026-10-25-C. Naming a blocking layer not on the list

Entered 25 October 2026 (failure protocol rule 4a). A layer not on the list is written "OTHER (named): [name]". Register wording may keep the short name in brackets. Applies: AB1-PL "OTHER (named): executive publication"; FA3b "OTHER (named): consolidation".
