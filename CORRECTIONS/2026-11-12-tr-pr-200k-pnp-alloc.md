Dated note, 12 November 2026: earlier allocation date inside the time-limited measure on TR-PR-200K-2027

This note sits on the already-live row TR-PR-200K-2027 (the 200,000-person time-limited measure). It is not a new row. No entry in data/entries.csv was edited (rule R1.2). The row's test and its scoring date of 31 Dec 2027 are unchanged. Nothing is scored by this note.

A new IRCC primary of 12 November 2026 adds an earlier date inside the existing measure. Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada, news release "Minister Metlege Diab highlights how Canada is working with provinces and territories to support worker retention", 12 November 2026: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/2026/11/minister-metlege-diab-highlights-how-canada-is-working-with-provinces-and-territories-to-support-worker-retention.html

The release reads: "As part of this time-limited measure, provinces and territories will be offered additional Provincial Nominee Program allocations and will be able to choose how much of this additional space they use to transition workers who are established in their communities and meeting their labour market needs. These allocations are intended to be used by March 31, 2027, to support the transition of workers and their families to permanent residence in 2027."

The same release reads: "offering the provinces and territories optional, additional space to transition up to a combined total of 100,000 workers and their families to permanent residence in 2027."

31 Mar 2027 is a date in the text: the allocations are "intended to be used by" that date. It is not a scoring date. The soft words are kept as printed: "intended to", "optional" and "up to". The workers figure, up to a combined total of 100,000, matches the row's "up to 100,000 workers".
