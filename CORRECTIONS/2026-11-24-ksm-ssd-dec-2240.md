On 18 November 2026 the Supreme Court of British Columbia issued Tsetsaut Skii Km Lax Ha Nation v British Columbia (Environment and Parks), 2026 BCSC 2240 (Madam Justice Burke), settling the terms of the 8 June 2026 order: https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/sc/26/22/2026BCSC2240.htm

The Court held: "it is evident in this case that the Court quashed the decision at issue."

The settled order states: "The July 25, 2024 determination of the Chief Executive Assessment Officer that the KSM Project has been substantially started is quashed and the Court remits it back to the Minister of Environment and Parks or their delegate for reconsideration."

Before that reconsideration: "the Crown shall engage in deep consultation with the Tsetsaut Skii km Lax Ha Nation, including the possible effects on the territory based on the Province's change to the consultation areas addressing the question of whether the project was substantially started." The same paragraph requires 90 days' notice for written submissions. The settled order sets no date by which that consultation or the reconsideration must be completed.

On reasonableness, the Court concluded: "it is unnecessary to decide the merits of the Tsetsaut Skii Km Lac Ha Nation's claim that the determination was unreasonable." Earlier in the reasons the Court wrote that June comments on reasonableness "should be considered obiter and not a determination on whether the SSD was reasonable or not." The SkeenaWild Conservation Trust petition "is dismissed without determination of the merits." The typo "Lac Ha" is in the official reasons and is kept in that quote.
