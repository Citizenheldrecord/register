R-2026-10-26-A. Provenance: posts and press are leads only

Entered 26 October 2026. Posts (on X or any other platform) and press reports are leads only. They can point the desk to a test, a date or a fact, but no row is scored on them alone. Every row must rest on a primary: a government, legislature, court, regulator, company or other first-hand publication by the body that made the decision or holds the record. A row whose date, fact or evidence rests only on a news report or post is flagged, and the flag carries a dated replacement task: the primary the desk will look for and the date by which it will look. If the primary is found, it is recorded in a new dated line naming the URL and the supporting words; the row's own cells are not edited. If no primary is found by the task date, that is recorded too, and the row is not scored on the report alone. Flags and replacements are listed in the CORRECTIONS folder.
